## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added
- Scans Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Movies, Music and Pictures and lists your files, grouped by type and sorted by size.
- Filters by folder, size, age and name.
- Moves selected files to the Trash. Nothing is deleted permanently, and Put Back works.
- About and Help windows, Check for Updates, and a link to support the project.
